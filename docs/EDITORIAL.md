# Editorial rules

The existing complete textbook is the read-only foundation. Supplementary volumes deepen domain knowledge through concrete design decisions, causal exposition, constraints, trade-offs, and verification. Follow IPA SA syllabus Ver.5.2 for coverage, but organize prose around technical relationships, not syllabus numbering. Separate factual claims about standards from illustrative scenarios. Provide primary references and edition details when relevant. Do not expose empty book chapters as published content.
